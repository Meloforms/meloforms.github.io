What to include

Helpful details make troubleshooting faster:

  • What you were doing and what happened.
  • Your device platform and Race to 100 version/build.
  • Your in-app Support ID, if available under Settings → Help & support.

Do not email passwords, full purchase receipts, challenge tokens, or other sensitive account information.
