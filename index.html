Rules in plain English

Select two dice and use addition, subtraction, multiplication, or exact division. The result replaces those two dice. Repeat until only one number remains.

A board is solved only when that final number equals the target. Every original die must be used, results cannot be negative, and division must produce a whole number.

An original die showing 1 has a 1↔10 badge, so it can become 10 and return to 1. A calculated 1 does not get that badge.

Quick answers

Still thinking?

What is Race to 100?

A fast arithmetic puzzle. Combine rolled numbers with +, −, ×, and ÷, use every original number, and solve targets 10 through 100.

Do I really have to use every number?

Yes. Reaching the target while another tile remains is not enough. Keep combining until one final number remains.

Why can a 1 become 10?

An original die that rolled 1 has a 1↔10 badge. You can flip that die before using it. A calculated result of 1 cannot flip.

Can a normal roll be impossible?

Normal gameplay rolls are checked by the game before they appear so at least one valid solution exists.

Can I play offline?

Solo, Relax, Climb, Sprint, and the tutorial work offline. The official Daily needs a connection for its first fetch and verification.
